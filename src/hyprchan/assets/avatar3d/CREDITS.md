Hyprchan's turquoise hair/eyes, lighting, blush, cheek deformation, white hoodie,
cords, sunshine emblem/clip, animation presets and native renderer are applied by
the code in `core/animations/avatar3d`. Original texture images are unmodified.
The hoodie shell is derived at runtime from the base's licensed shirt topology:
subdivision, extended sleeves, fitted proportions, bust shaping and folds. The
hood, lining, cords and rounded W badge are generated by the project. The VRM
file itself is unchanged.

## Hyprland hairpin

`hyprland-mark.svg` extracts the unmodified logo outline (path `st7`) and its
blue/turquoise gradient from the official Hyprland header. The viewport is
cropped to the mark and the wordmark is omitted. The mesh follows a centerline
derived from that contour, thickened into a rounded, woven cord with soft toon
shading. It is attached to the head bone opposite the original gold sparkle.
The logo's narrow tips are shortened to keep the opening clear at this thickness.
